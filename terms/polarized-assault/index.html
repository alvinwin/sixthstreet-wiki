Combat mechanic

Polarized Assault

Alice's named Assault mechanic, which can deal Assault damage without following the normal buildup trigger.

2 linked sources checked 2026-08-20

Definition

How it works

  1. Alice meets the skill-specific conditions for Polarized Assault.
  2. The effect ignores normal Anomaly Buildup progress.
  3. It deals damage equivalent to regular Assault and can participate in Disorder interactions.

Why it matters

What it changes

Polarized Assault is an exception to the normal fill-the-gauge trigger flow and should not be generalized to other Agents or treated as a system-wide “Polarize” category.

No normal buildup trigger
The skill can produce its Assault-equivalent effect without filling the Physical buildup gauge in the ordinary way.
Agent-specific rule
Its activation and scaling belong to Alice's kit, not the base Attribute Anomaly rules.

Common confusion

Polarized is not Polarity

Polarized Assault is Alice's mechanic. Polarity Disorder is a different special mechanic associated with other Agents and does not remove the active anomaly.
